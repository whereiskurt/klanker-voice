## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-16-09-01 | Denial of service | the public voice service and the DIDs are genuinely down for the duration of the round trip | high | mitigate | A backup is taken first; the window is bounded by the roughly five-minute round trip; the operator chooses the timing; and resume is verified end to end with a real conversation and a real call before the checkpoint closes. |
| T-16-09-02 | Tampering | a plan that proposes destroying an ALB, a bucket, or a table instead of only scaling | high | mitigate | Task 1 makes a no-destroy plan an explicit acceptance criterion and a stop-the-line finding, checked before anything is committed. |
| T-16-09-03 | Tampering | a failed pause that reports success — the exact §5.2 hazard | high | mitigate | Task 2 independently verifies zero tasks with `aws ecs describe-services` rather than trusting kv's own report, and records whether the correction fired. |
| T-16-09-04 | Elevation of privilege | the kill-switch silently changing state across the round trip | high | mitigate | The kill-switch value is recorded three times — before, during, and after — and byte-identity is an acceptance criterion (D-24). |
| T-16-09-05 | Tampering | an unrelated working-tree change riding along in the pause commit | medium | mitigate | The commit scope is verified after the fact with `git log`, asserting each commit touches only `site.hcl`. |
| T-16-09-06 | Denial of service | a resume that never reaches healthy, leaving the stack down past the intended window | medium | mitigate | The health wait is bounded and reports per-group state; the runbook's troubleshooting section covers this case and the drain-correction path can be run manually. |
</threat_model>
